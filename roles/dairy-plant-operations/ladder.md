# Dairy Plant Operations — Career Ladder

## Calibration summary

- **Variant:** general (operational / trade role) — a single, unified individual-contributor to manager progression across **seven levels, L1–L7**.
- **Unified IC→manager ladder:** operator craft and operational leadership advance in ONE ladder rather than splitting into separate IC and manager tracks. **L4 Lead Operator is the IC-terminal master operator** who leads the floor without the title; L5–L7 are operational leadership (Shift Supervisor → Area / Production Manager → Plant Operations Manager).
- **Scope widens by breadth of responsibility:** one machine / task → a process area → multiple areas → a line end to end → a shift crew → a department across shifts → the whole plant.
- **Leadership as informal proxy, then formal:** leadership competencies show as informal, demonstrable-now behaviors at the IC levels (trains a peer, runs the pre-shift huddle, calls the safety stand-down, owns changeover discipline) and formalize at L5–L7. Conversely, food-safety, process, and equipment craft keep advancing through L7 — a plant manager still owns the HACCP plan and the PSM program.
- **Catalog mapping:** **13** competencies map to existing OCF capabilities (generic operations, maintenance, EHS, leadership, finance); **25** dairy/food-plant craft competencies reference pending **FBM (Food & Beverage Manufacturing)** proposals under `contrib/` (FBM-01 … FBM-25).
- **Proficiency curve (P1–P6):** craft competencies climb L1:P1 → L7:P6 with an L4–L5 depth plateau at the IC-terminal master-operator bar (depth holds at P4 while scope widens from a line to a crew); leadership competencies start as informal proxies (L1:P1) and rise to P6 at plant-lead scope.

**Structure:** 6 key areas · 17 focus areas (each 2+ competencies) · 38 competencies · 7 levels (L1–L7).

## Level overview

| Level | Title | Scope band | Focus |
|-------|-------|------------|-------|
| L1 | Operator I | one machine / task under direction | learns the plant and runs a single process area under direction |
| L2 | Operator II | a process area across a shift | runs a process area independently across a full shift |
| L3 | Senior Operator | multiple process areas | operates multiple areas, troubleshoots, and coaches peers |
| L4 | Lead Operator | a line / process end to end (IC terminal) | owns a line end to end and leads the floor without the title — IC terminal |
| L5 | Shift Supervisor | a shift crew | leads a shift crew and its safety, quality, and output |
| L6 | Area / Production Manager | a department across shifts | owns a department across shifts — its people, cost, and performance |
| L7 | Plant Operations Manager | the whole plant | owns whole-plant operations, safety, quality, and cost |
