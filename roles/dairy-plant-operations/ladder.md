# Dairy Plant Operations (L1-L7) - Canonical — Career Ladder

Unified individual-contributor-to-manager career ladder (L1-L7) for a Grade A dairy processing plant, where food-safety, process, and equipment craft and operational leadership advance together from Operator I to Plant Operations Manager.


| Level | Title | Scope | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Operator I | one machine / task under direction | learns the plant and runs a single process area under direction |
| L2 | Operator II | a process area across a shift | runs a process area independently across a full shift |
| L3 | Senior Operator | multiple process areas | operates multiple areas, troubleshoots, and coaches peers |
| L4 | Lead Operator | a line / process end to end (IC terminal) | owns a line end to end and leads the floor without the title - IC terminal |
| L5 | Shift Supervisor | a shift crew | leads a shift crew and its safety, quality, and output |
| L6 | Area / Production Manager | a department across shifts | owns a department across shifts - its people, cost, and performance |
| L7 | Plant Operations Manager | the whole plant | owns whole-plant operations, safety, quality, and cost |

## Food Safety & Regulatory Compliance

#### Thermal Process Control

##### Pasteurization & flow-diversion control

*Anchor:* U.S. FDA / IMS, Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO), 2019 Revision — the flow-diversion device and its legal setpoints are the plant's public-health guarantee

| OCF | L1 | L2 | L3 | L4 | L5 | L6 | L7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [FBM-01](../../data/capabilities.md#fbm-01) — Pasteurization & flow-diversion control | [P1](../../data/proficiency_scale.md#p1) | [P2](../../data/proficiency_scale.md#p2) | [P3](../../data/proficiency_scale.md#p3) | [P4](../../data/proficiency_scale.md#p4) | [P4](../../data/proficiency_scale.md#p4) | [P5](../../data/proficiency_scale.md#p5) | [P6](../../data/proficiency_scale.md#p6) |

- **L1 (= P1)** — Under direction, confirms the flow-diversion device holds forward flow only when temperature is above the cut-in set point and diverts below it; reads the diversion behavior off the recorder; and escalates any diversion that will not clear or any FDD anomaly to a lead. *Evidenced by:* States the legal cut-in temperature for the product running. *Scope:* one machine / task under direction. *Why this level:* FBM-01 P1 ('confirms the FDD holds forward flow only above cut-in...escalates any anomaly to a lead') is Dreyfus's novice band exactly matched to L1's scope (one machine, work checked): the PMO's cut-in/cut-out set points are given, not judged. (Dreyfus & Dreyfus, A Five-Stage Model of the Mental Activities Involved in Directed Skill Acquisition (1980); U.S. FDA / IMS, Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO), 2019 Revision)
- **L2 (= P2)** — Independently controls the flow-diversion function — confirms cut-in and cut-out act at the legal set points, verifies forward flow only above cut-in, and handles routine nuisance diversions and short stops so that only properly treated product advances. *Evidenced by:* Clears a routine divert and records its cause unprompted. *Scope:* a process area across a shift. *Why this level:* FBM-01 P2 ('independently controls the flow-diversion function...handles routine nuisance diversions') is Dreyfus's advanced-beginner band at L2's process-area-across-a-shift scope: the legal set points are applied unsupervised for the routine case. (Dreyfus & Dreyfus, A Five-Stage Model of the Mental Activities Involved in Directed Skill Acquisition (1980); U.S. FDA / IMS, Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO), 2019 Revision)
- **L3 (= P3)** — Troubleshoots the common causes of nuisance diversion and failure-to-divert (sensor drift, low product level, restart transients), restores correct divert/forward behavior without compromising the treatment, and coaches operators on FDD behavior and the legal cut-in/cut-out set points. *Evidenced by:* Cracks a nuisance-divert pattern others cannot explain. *Scope:* multiple process areas. *Why this level:* FBM-01 P3 ('troubleshoots...sensor drift, low product level, restart transients...coaches operators') is the Dreyfus competent band at L3's multiple-areas scope: diagnosing why the kill step misbehaves, not just running it. (Dreyfus & Dreyfus, A Five-Stage Model of the Mental Activities Involved in Directed Skill Acquisition (1980); U.S. FDA / IMS, Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO), 2019 Revision)
- **L4 (= P4)** — Sets the local diversion-control standard — writes the diversion-response procedure others follow, defines the cut-in/cut-out set points and the FDD seal-and-wiring integrity checks at the machine — and is the person called when a diversion won't clear. *Evidenced by:* Writes the divert-response procedure the line follows and teaches the chart to new operators. *Scope:* a line / process end to end (IC terminal). *Why this level:* FBM-01 P4 ('sets the local diversion-control standard...is the person called when a diversion won't clear') is SFIA's Ensure/advise band -- the Lead Operator (IC-terminal, a line end to end) authors the line's divert-response procedure. (SFIA Foundation, Skills Framework for the Information Age, version 9 (2024); U.S. FDA / IMS, Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO), 2019 Revision)
- **L5 (= P4)** — Sets the local diversion-control standard — writes the diversion-response procedure others follow, defines the cut-in/cut-out set points and the FDD seal-and-wiring integrity checks at the machine — and is the person called when a diversion won't clear. *Evidenced by:* Surfaces crew-wide pasteurization compliance in the shift review. *Scope:* a shift crew. *Why this level:* L5 holds the same P4 depth as L4 -- FBM-01's P4 bar is already the master-operator standard; Shift Supervisor's step is scope (a shift crew), auditing every operator's chart against the standard L4 already sets, not a deeper technical bar. (SFIA Foundation, Skills Framework for the Information Age, version 9 (2024); U.S. FDA / IMS, Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO), 2019 Revision)
- **L6 (= P5)** — Governs flow-diversion control across the plant's thermal lines — owns the FDD test-and-seal regime, the cut-in/cut-out control standard, and the trending that drives out chronic diversions — and sets the competency bar for who is cleared to hold the kill step. *Evidenced by:* Drives divert rate down department-wide with a targeted FDD test-and-seal standard. *Scope:* a department across shifts. *Why this level:* FBM-01 P5 ('governs flow-diversion control across the plant's thermal lines...sets the competency bar for who is cleared to hold the kill step') is SFIA's Initiate/influence band at L6's department-across-shifts scope. (SFIA Foundation, Skills Framework for the Information Age, version 9 (2024); U.S. FDA / IMS, Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO), 2019 Revision)
- **L7 (= P6)** — Defines flow-diversion-control practice adopted beyond the plant — advances how the divert/forward decision and legal cut-in/cut-out are controlled and safeguarded — and is a reference others in the industry adopt. *Evidenced by:* Signs the PMO compliance the plant is inspected against. *Scope:* the whole plant. *Why this level:* FBM-01 P6 ('defines flow-diversion-control practice adopted beyond the plant') is SFIA's Set-strategy/inspire band -- the Plant Operations Manager signs the PMO compliance the whole plant is inspected against. (SFIA Foundation, Skills Framework for the Information Age, version 9 (2024); U.S. FDA / IMS, Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO), 2019 Revision)

##### Thermal-process legal limits & records

*Anchor:* U.S. FDA / IMS, Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO), 2019 Revision — the legally defensible thermal process and its records are what an inspector audits

- **L1 (= P1)** — Under direction, records the required time and temperature for the product being run against the posted legal minimum, files the charts and logs completely and legibly, and flags a record that does not show the process was met. *Evidenced by:* Reads the required minimum hold temperature off the process record. *Scope:* one machine / task under direction. *Why this level:* FBM-02 P1 ('records the required time and temperature...flags a record that does not show the process was met') is Dreyfus's novice band at L1's one-unit, checked scope: transcription against a legal minimum someone else set. (Dreyfus & Dreyfus, A Five-Stage Model of the Mental Activities Involved in Directed Skill Acquisition (1980); U.S. FDA / IMS, Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO), 2019 Revision)
- **L2 (= P2)** — Independently confirms the running process against the correct legal minimum for each product, completes and reconciles the thermal-process records for a shift, and knows which reading proves the process and which does not. *Evidenced by:* Catches and reports an out-of-limit hold time without prompting. *Scope:* a process area across a shift. *Why this level:* FBM-02 P2 ('independently confirms the running process against the correct legal minimum...knows which reading proves the process') is Dreyfus's advanced-beginner band at L2's shift scope. (Dreyfus & Dreyfus, A Five-Stage Model of the Mental Activities Involved in Directed Skill Acquisition (1980); U.S. FDA / IMS, Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO), 2019 Revision)
- **L3 (= P3)** — Owns the thermal-process records for a line or area — verifies every batch's record shows the legal minimum was met, resolves gaps and discrepancies before they age, and coaches operators on why a specific time/temperature is the legal floor. *Evidenced by:* Defends a borderline record with the supporting evidence. *Scope:* multiple process areas. *Why this level:* FBM-02 P3 ('owns the thermal-process records for a line or area...resolves gaps and discrepancies before they age') is the Dreyfus competent band at L3's multiple-areas scope -- autonomous judgment on what makes a record legally defensible. (Dreyfus & Dreyfus, A Five-Stage Model of the Mental Activities Involved in Directed Skill Acquisition (1980); U.S. FDA / IMS, Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO), 2019 Revision)
- **L4 (= P4)** — Establishes the documented scheduled thermal process and legal minima others run to — sets the record requirements, the review-of-records routine, and what constitutes a defensible batch record — and is the person who signs that a lot's process is proven. *Evidenced by:* Sets the recordkeeping standard an inspector can follow end to end. *Scope:* a line / process end to end (IC terminal). *Why this level:* FBM-02 P4 ('establishes the documented scheduled thermal process...is the person who signs that a lot's process is proven') is SFIA's Ensure/advise band -- the Lead Operator authors the line's record standard, IC terminal. (SFIA Foundation, Skills Framework for the Information Age, version 9 (2024); U.S. FDA / IMS, Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO), 2019 Revision)
- **L5 (= P4)** — Establishes the documented scheduled thermal process and legal minima others run to — sets the record requirements, the review-of-records routine, and what constitutes a defensible batch record — and is the person who signs that a lot's process is proven. *Evidenced by:* Signs off the crew's thermal records as inspection-ready. *Scope:* a shift crew. *Why this level:* L5 keeps L4's P4 depth -- the Shift Supervisor audits the crew's records against the standard the Lead Operator already authored; scope widens to a crew, the record-authority bar does not deepen. (SFIA Foundation, Skills Framework for the Information Age, version 9 (2024); U.S. FDA / IMS, Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO), 2019 Revision)
- **L6 (= P5)** — Governs the thermal-process-authority program across the plant — owns the process filings and validations, the record-retention and review standard, and the escalation for a shorted process — and defends the minima and records to a regulator or auditor without gaps. *Evidenced by:* Closes record gaps before an audit finds them. *Scope:* a department across shifts. *Why this level:* FBM-02 P5 ('governs the thermal-process-authority program across the plant...defends the minima and records to a regulator or auditor without gaps') is SFIA's Initiate/influence band at L6's department scope. (SFIA Foundation, Skills Framework for the Information Age, version 9 (2024); U.S. FDA / IMS, Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO), 2019 Revision)
- **L7 (= P6)** — Sets thermal-process-legality practice others adopt — advances how legal minima are established, validated, and documented — and is a recognized authority whose recordkeeping standard is emulated. *Evidenced by:* Is the plant's authority on thermal-process legality. *Scope:* the whole plant. *Why this level:* FBM-02 P6 ('sets thermal-process-legality practice others adopt') is SFIA's Set-strategy/inspire band -- the plant's authority on thermal-process legality. (SFIA Foundation, Skills Framework for the Information Age, version 9 (2024); U.S. FDA / IMS, Grade "A" Pasteurized Milk Ordinance (PMO), 2019 Revision)

#### Food Safety Systems

##### HACCP plan & CCP monitoring

*Anchor:* Codex Alimentarius Commission, General Principles of Food Hygiene (CXC 1-1969) and its HACCP Annex — CCP monitoring is the systematic guard against food-safety hazards

- **L1 (= P1)** — Under direction, monitors an assigned critical control point on schedule, records the reading against its critical limit, and immediately notifies a lead and holds product when a limit is exceeded. *Evidenced by:* Knows which step on the line is a CCP. *Scope:* one machine / task under direction. *Why this level:* FBM-03 P1 ('monitors an assigned CCP on schedule...immediately notifies a lead and holds product') is Dreyfus's novice band at L1's one-CCP, checked scope -- executing Codex's monitoring principle exactly. (Dreyfus & Dreyfus, A Five-Stage Model of the Mental Activities Involved in Directed Skill Acquisition (1980); Codex Alimentarius Commission, General Principles of Food Hygiene (CXC 1-1969) and its HACCP Annex)
- **L2 (= P2)** — Independently monitors the critical control points for a line across a shift, recognizes a deviation as it happens, initiates the documented corrective action, and completes the monitoring and corrective-action records. *Evidenced by:* Takes the first corrective action on a CCP deviation without waiting. *Scope:* a process area across a shift. *Why this level:* FBM-03 P2 ('independently monitors the CCPs for a line...initiates the documented corrective action') is Dreyfus's advanced-beginner band at L2's shift scope. (Dreyfus & Dreyfus, A Five-Stage Model of the Mental Activities Involved in Directed Skill Acquisition (1980); Codex Alimentarius Commission, General Principles of Food Hygiene (CXC 1-1969) and its HACCP Annex)
- **L3 (= P3)** — Owns CCP monitoring for an area — verifies monitoring is happening as written, investigates the cause of a deviation, coaches operators on critical limits and corrective actions, and handles the non-routine deviation without a supervisor. *Evidenced by:* Judges a borderline CCP reading correctly under pressure. *Scope:* multiple process areas. *Why this level:* FBM-03 P3 ('verifies monitoring is happening as written...handles the non-routine deviation without a supervisor') is the Dreyfus competent band at L3's multiple-areas scope -- NACMCF's corrective-action principle exercised on the ambiguous case. (Dreyfus & Dreyfus, A Five-Stage Model of the Mental Activities Involved in Directed Skill Acquisition (1980); National Advisory Committee on Microbiological Criteria for Foods, Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Point Principles and Application Guidelines, Journal of Food Protection 61(9), 1246-1259 (1998))
- **L4 (= P4)** — Builds the HACCP plan for a process — conducts the hazard analysis, determines the CCPs and critical limits, writes the monitoring and corrective-action procedures others follow, and leads reassessment when the process changes. *Evidenced by:* Writes the CCP-monitoring procedure the line runs to and teaches it to the crew. *Scope:* a line / process end to end (IC terminal). *Why this level:* FBM-03 P4 ('builds the HACCP plan...writes the monitoring and corrective-action procedures others follow') is SFIA's Ensure/advise band -- the Lead Operator authors the line's HACCP plan, IC terminal. (SFIA Foundation, Skills Framework for the Information Age, version 9 (2024); National Advisory Committee on Microbiological Criteria for Foods, Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Point Principles and Application Guidelines, Journal of Food Protection 61(9), 1246-1259 (1998))
- **L5 (= P4)** — Builds the HACCP plan for a process — conducts the hazard analysis, determines the CCPs and critical limits, writes the monitoring and corrective-action procedures others follow, and leads reassessment when the process changes. *Evidenced by:* Signs the crew's CCP verification records. *Scope:* a shift crew. *Why this level:* L5 keeps L4's P4 plan-authorship depth -- the Shift Supervisor verifies the crew's monitoring and owns corrective-action closure against the plan L4 already built; scope, not depth, widens to a crew. (SFIA Foundation, Skills Framework for the Information Age, version 9 (2024); National Advisory Committee on Microbiological Criteria for Foods, Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Point Principles and Application Guidelines, Journal of Food Protection 61(9), 1246-1259 (1998))
- **L6 (= P5)** — Governs the HACCP program across the plant — leads the HACCP team, owns validation of critical limits, the verification and reassessment schedule, and the record-review discipline — and drives systemic corrective action from deviation trends. *Evidenced by:* Catches a hazard the current plan misses before it reaches product. *Scope:* a department across shifts. *Why this level:* FBM-03 P5 ('governs the HACCP program across the plant...leads the HACCP team, owns validation...and reassessment') is SFIA's Initiate/influence band at L6's department scope. (SFIA Foundation, Skills Framework for the Information Age, version 9 (2024); National Advisory Committee on Microbiological Criteria for Foods, Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Point Principles and Application Guidelines, Journal of Food Protection 61(9), 1246-1259 (1998))
- **L7 (= P6)** — Advances HACCP practice beyond the plant — shapes how hazard analysis and CCP determination are done — and is a recognized authority whose plans and methods others adopt. *Evidenced by:* Is accountable for the plant's HACCP validity at audit. *Scope:* the whole plant. *Why this level:* FBM-03 P6 ('advances HACCP practice beyond the plant') is SFIA's Set-strategy/inspire band -- accountable for the plant's HACCP validity at audit. (SFIA Foundation, Skills Framework for the Information Age, version 9 (2024); Codex Alimentarius Commission, General Principles of Food Hygiene (CXC 1-1969) and its HACCP Annex)

*Anchor:* Horngren, Datar & Rajan, Horngren's Cost Accounting: A Managerial Emphasis, 17th ed. (Pearson, 2023) — cost and labor ownership is how operations are held to budget

| OCF | L1 | L2 | L3 | L4 | L5 | L6 | L7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [EM-06](../../data/capabilities.md#em-06) — Budget & vendor management | [P1](../../data/proficiency_scale.md#p1) | [P1](../../data/proficiency_scale.md#p1) | [P2](../../data/proficiency_scale.md#p2) | [P3](../../data/proficiency_scale.md#p3) | [P4](../../data/proficiency_scale.md#p4) | [P5](../../data/proficiency_scale.md#p5) | [P6](../../data/proficiency_scale.md#p6) |

- **L1 (= P1)** — Tracks spend and processes vendor invoices under guidance. *Evidenced by:* Logs yield and labor data accurately for the shift. *Scope:* one machine / task under direction. *Why this level:* EM-06 P1 ('tracks spend and processes vendor invoices under guidance') is Dreyfus's novice band -- Operator I logs yield and labor data accurately for the shift, at L1's checked scope. (Dreyfus & Dreyfus, A Five-Stage Model of the Mental Activities Involved in Directed Skill Acquisition (1980); Horngren, Datar & Rajan, Horngren's Cost Accounting: A Managerial Emphasis, 17th ed. (Pearson, 2023))
- **L2 (= P1)** — Tracks spend and processes vendor invoices under guidance. *Evidenced by:* Points out a waste or overtime driver on their area. *Scope:* a process area across a shift. *Why this level:* L2 holds EM-06's P1 depth -- Operator II is independent on their own station, but pointing out a waste or overtime driver is still tracking/reporting, short of P2's managing a budget with normal review. (Dreyfus & Dreyfus, A Five-Stage Model of the Mental Activities Involved in Directed Skill Acquisition (1980); Horngren, Datar & Rajan, Horngren's Cost Accounting: A Managerial Emphasis, 17th ed. (Pearson, 2023))
- **L3 (= P2)** — Manages a team budget and standard vendor relationships with normal review. *Evidenced by:* Cuts a controllable cost on the floor unprompted. *Scope:* multiple process areas. *Why this level:* EM-06 P2 ('manages a team budget and standard vendor relationships with normal review') is Dreyfus's advanced-beginner band at L3's multiple-areas scope -- cutting a controllable cost on the floor unprompted, still within a set budget someone else owns. (Dreyfus & Dreyfus, A Five-Stage Model of the Mental Activities Involved in Directed Skill Acquisition (1980); Horngren, Datar & Rajan, Horngren's Cost Accounting: A Managerial Emphasis, 17th ed. (Pearson, 2023))
- **L4 (= P3)** — Independently controls budgets under pressure and negotiates difficult vendor terms. *Evidenced by:* Sets the yield and labor-efficiency standard the line runs to. *Scope:* a line / process end to end (IC terminal). *Why this level:* EM-06 P3 ('independently controls budgets under pressure and negotiates difficult vendor terms') is the Dreyfus competent band -- Lead Operator sets the yield and labor-efficiency standard the line runs to, IC terminal. (Dreyfus & Dreyfus, A Five-Stage Model of the Mental Activities Involved in Directed Skill Acquisition (1980); Horngren, Datar & Rajan, Horngren's Cost Accounting: A Managerial Emphasis, 17th ed. (Pearson, 2023))
- **L5 (= P4)** — Defines budget and vendor practices others adopt and rescues failing vendor relationships. *Evidenced by:* Manages the crew to its labor and cost targets. *Scope:* a shift crew. *Why this level:* EM-06 P4 ('defines budget and vendor practices others adopt and rescues failing vendor relationships') is SFIA's Ensure/advise band -- Shift Supervisor formally manages the crew to its labor and cost targets, owning a real budget line for the first time. (SFIA Foundation, Skills Framework for the Information Age, version 9 (2024); Horngren, Datar & Rajan, Horngren's Cost Accounting: A Managerial Emphasis, 17th ed. (Pearson, 2023))
- **L6 (= P5)** — Sets org-wide budget and vendor strategy and anticipates cost and supplier risks. *Evidenced by:* Drives a labor-variance improvement other areas adopt. *Scope:* a department across shifts. *Why this level:* EM-06 P5 ('sets org-wide budget and vendor strategy and anticipates cost and supplier risks') is SFIA's Initiate/influence band at L6's department scope -- driving a labor-variance improvement other areas adopt. (SFIA Foundation, Skills Framework for the Information Age, version 9 (2024); Horngren, Datar & Rajan, Horngren's Cost Accounting: A Managerial Emphasis, 17th ed. (Pearson, 2023))
- **L7 (= P6)** — Defines exemplary budget and vendor management for the field and influences practice. *Evidenced by:* Is accountable for the plant's operations P&L. *Scope:* the whole plant. *Why this level:* EM-06 P6 ('defines exemplary budget and vendor management for the field and influences practice') is SFIA's Set-strategy/inspire band -- accountable for the plant's operations P&L. (SFIA Foundation, Skills Framework for the Information Age, version 9 (2024); Horngren, Datar & Rajan, Horngren's Cost Accounting: A Managerial Emphasis, 17th ed. (Pearson, 2023))
