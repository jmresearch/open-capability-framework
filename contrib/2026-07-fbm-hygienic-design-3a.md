# Hygienic design & 3-A conformance

- **Proposed id:** FBM-19
- **Domain / focus area:** Food & Beverage Manufacturing / Sanitation
- **Type:** Technical
- **Description:** Applies hygienic (3-A/EHEDG) design and installation principles so equipment is cleanable, drainable, and does not harbor pathogens.
- **Why it's missing:** The role judges and installs equipment for cleanability — drainability, self-cleaning geometry, food-contact materials and finishes, elimination of dead legs and harborage niches — against consensus hygienic-design standards (3-A, EHEDG), the design-side complement to actually cleaning it. SCM-09 Asset & maintenance management covers keeping equipment running, not designing/installing it to be cleanable; WPL-02 Building operations & maintenance is facility upkeep, not hygienic equipment design; QA-04 Risk-based quality frames quality decisions but carries none of the hygienic-design competence. None is sanitation- or food-safety-specific.

## P1–P6 behavioral profile

- **P1 (Assisted):** Checks equipment against a hygienic-design checklist under direction — identifies obvious cleanability problems (dead legs, rough welds, non-drainable low points, unsealed threads) and flags them rather than signing off.
- **P2 (Independent):** Applies hygienic-design principles independently on routine work — installs and reassembles equipment to drain and self-clean, selects food-contact-legal materials and gaskets, and checks fittings and welds against the hygienic standard before returning to service.
- **P3 (Proficient):** Troubleshoots cleanability and harborage for the area — finds why a spot won't clean or a niche harbors organisms, corrects dead legs, cross-contamination points, and poor drainage, and coaches on hygienic installation and reassembly.
- **P4 (Expert):** Sets the hygienic-design and installation standard the area follows — specifies the 3-A/EHEDG conformance, material, finish, drainage, and cleanability requirements new and modified equipment must meet, and reviews installations against them before start-up.
- **P5 (Authority):** Governs hygienic design for the department — owns the equipment-design and acceptance standards, drives out harborage and cleanability defects across the process, and defends hygienic-design conformance in audits and equipment procurement.
- **P6 (Pioneer):** Defines hygienic-design practice beyond the plant — sets cleanability and 3-A/EHEDG conformance methods other sites adopt and advances the practice in standards and industry forums.
