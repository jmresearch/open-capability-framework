# Wastewater & effluent compliance

- **Proposed id:** FBM-23
- **Domain / focus area:** Food & Beverage Manufacturing / Plant Utilities
- **Type:** Technical
- **Description:** Operating pretreatment and effluent systems so plant discharge stays within permit limits (Clean Water Act / NPDES or the local pretreatment authority), including the sampling and reporting that prove it.
- **Why it's missing:** A dairy plant's high-strength, high-BOD, fat- and cleaning-chemical-laden effluent must be pretreated and discharged within a permit, with self-monitoring records the authority can audit. The nearest catalog capabilities do not cover operating an effluent system to permit: WPL-08 Environmental health & safety frames environmental compliance broadly but not the operating craft of a DAF, equalization, pH, and biological pretreatment train; WPL-02 Building operations & maintenance covers building utilities, not a regulated discharge; QA-04 Risk-based quality is product quality, not effluent. None is wastewater-and-effluent specific.

## P1–P6 behavioral profile

- **P1 (Assisted):** Reads and logs pH, flow, and level on the effluent system, pulls the routine composite or grab sample as directed, doses chemical to setpoint, and reports an out-of-range pH or an upset to a qualified operator.
- **P2 (Independent):** Runs the pretreatment system across a shift independently — manages equalization, pH correction, fat/solids removal (e.g. DAF), and chemical dosing to hold effluent within permit — and takes, preserves, and records the self-monitoring samples correctly.
- **P3 (Proficient):** Troubleshoots the effluent train when a parameter drifts toward the limit — traces a BOD, FOG, TSS, or pH excursion back to a plant-side source such as a product dump or CIP slug, corrects it, and coaches operators on load-smoothing and sampling discipline.
- **P4 (Expert):** Sets the operating procedures, dosing, and sampling practice for the effluent system, manages the plant to stay inside its permit envelope, and owns the accuracy and completeness of the discharge-monitoring records an inspector will read.
- **P5 (Authority):** Governs wastewater compliance plant-wide — the permit relationship, source-reduction and load-management program, and the monitoring-and-reporting system — and drives the improvements that cut discharge strength, surcharge cost, and permit risk.
- **P6 (Pioneer):** Sets effluent-management practice beyond the site — source-reduction, water-reuse, and resource-recovery approaches and the compliance-operations standards other plants and the industry adopt.
