# Separation & standardization

- **Proposed id:** FBM-11
- **Domain / focus area:** Food & Beverage Manufacturing / Raw Material Handling
- **Type:** Technical
- **Description:** Operates separators and standardizes fat and solids to target through separation and blending control so every stream meets its compositional specification.
- **Why it's missing:** The role runs continuous centrifugal separation and fat/solids standardization — a component-balance craft (separation efficiency, blend ratios, yield/giveaway, mass reconciliation) specific to dairy processing. SCM-07 Manufacturing & production operations describes running production generically but nothing about separator operation or standardizing a compositional target; SCM-08 Production scheduling sequences work but does not touch component control; OPS-04 Reliability & Production Operations is uptime/reliability framed, not the compositional-control competence. None is dairy-process-specific.

## P1–P6 behavioral profile

- **P1 (Assisted):** Starts, feeds, and shuts down the separator on the checklist under direction; records cream and skim readings and the standardization target, and calls for help when a reading drifts off target.
- **P2 (Independent):** Runs separation and standardization independently across a shift — sets and holds the fat/solids target by adjusting separation and blend ratios, and verifies the finished stream to spec by test.
- **P3 (Proficient):** Troubleshoots the separator and standardization loop for the line — corrects poor separation efficiency, off-target fat, and desludge/backpressure problems, reconciles component mass balance, and coaches operators on holding target through feed swings.
- **P4 (Expert):** Sets the standardization procedure and targets the line follows — defines the blend calculations, control limits, and verification testing, and dials in separator setup so fat/solids and yield hold consistently across products and operators.
- **P5 (Authority):** Governs standardization and component recovery for the department — sets the fat/solids specifications and yield/loss targets, drives improvement in separation efficiency and giveaway, and owns the mass-balance accountability.
- **P6 (Pioneer):** Defines separation and standardization practice beyond the plant — sets component-control and yield methods other sites adopt and advances the practice in industry forums.
