# Cold chain & finished-goods integrity

- **Proposed id:** FBM-15
- **Domain / focus area:** Food & Beverage Manufacturing / Packaging & Cold Chain
- **Type:** Technical
- **Description:** Protects finished-product temperature and integrity from fill through cold storage and dispatch so product ships within its cold-chain and shelf-life requirements.
- **Why it's missing:** The role owns the finished-goods cold chain — post-fill cooling, refrigerated-storage temperature, FEFO rotation, hold/release, and trailer pre-cool/load integrity at dispatch — a food-safety- and shelf-life-critical craft under Grade A cold-holding requirements. The nearest catalog capabilities are the storage-and-move pair: SCM-06 (Warehousing) owns storage, rotation, and hold/release, and SCM-11 (Logistics & distribution) owns dispatch and outbound transport — but neither carries the food cold-chain temperature integrity that is the delta here: holding finished product within a food-safety and shelf-life temperature envelope from fill through the dock, and dispositioning product on a temperature excursion. (SCM-07 Manufacturing & production operations ends at making product; WPL-02 Building operations & maintenance covers keeping the facility running, including refrigeration plant, not finished-goods temperature integrity and disposition; OPS-04 Reliability & Production Operations is uptime-framed.) None is food-safety- or cold-chain-specific.

## P1–P6 behavioral profile

- **P1 (Assisted):** Follows the cold-chain checklist under direction — records cooler and product temperatures, checks that finished goods move promptly into refrigerated storage, and reports an out-of-range temperature or damaged pallet instead of shipping it.
- **P2 (Independent):** Manages finished-goods cold chain independently across a shift — monitors storage and load-out temperatures, enforces stock rotation and hold/release, verifies trailer pre-cool and load integrity at dispatch, and holds product that breaks temperature.
- **P3 (Proficient):** Troubleshoots cold-chain and finished-goods integrity for the area — resolves temperature-excursion, rotation, and load-integrity problems, dispositions affected product, traces the cause of a cold-chain break, and coaches on load-out and hold/release discipline.
- **P4 (Expert):** Sets the cold-chain and finished-goods procedure the area follows — defines the temperature limits, monitoring points, rotation/FEFO rules, and excursion-disposition criteria from fill through dispatch that others run to.
- **P5 (Authority):** Governs finished-goods cold-chain integrity for the department — owns the temperature-control and traceability standards from production to shipping, drives shelf-life and shrink/excursion improvement, and defends cold-chain records in audits and customer/regulatory review.
- **P6 (Pioneer):** Defines finished-goods cold-chain practice beyond the plant — sets temperature-integrity and traceability methods other sites adopt and advances the practice in industry forums.
