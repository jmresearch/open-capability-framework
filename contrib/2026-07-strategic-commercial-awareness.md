# Strategic & commercial awareness

- **Proposed id:** PD-07
- **Domain / focus area:** Product Delivery / Execution
- **Type:** Behavioral
- **Description:** Frames technical decisions in economic and business terms — cost, revenue risk, cost of delay, unit economics — and answers for whether the work actually moved its business outcome.
- **Why it's missing:** Two runs of the full-stack-typescript canonical consolidation carried this as a competency distinct from product thinking (anchors: Rumelt, Good Strategy Bad Strategy; Accelerate). PD-06 (Product Thinking) connects work to user and product value but not to commercial framing and outcome accountability; PD-02 (Prioritization & Economic Thinking) applies economics to sequencing work items, not to owning business results of technical strategy. Staff-plus IC leveling at major companies is explicitly impact-denominated, so the ladder needs a ratable home for it from entry level up.

## P1–P6 behavioral profile

- **P1 (Assisted):** States what business outcome their current task serves — and asks when they can't — treating infrastructure and license costs as real money.
- **P2 (Independent):** Checks whether shipped work actually moved its metric and says so plainly when it didn't; weighs cost and maintenance burden in the technical choices they own.
- **P3 (Proficient):** Frames a domain's technical decisions in business terms — cost of delay, revenue risk, support load — pushes back on work with weak business rationale, and redirects effort when the numbers say the plan is wrong.
- **P4 (Expert):** Builds the business case for the cross-team technical investments with the largest return and is trusted by leadership to have done the math.
- **P5 (Authority):** Aligns an organization's technical strategy with its economics — cost curves, velocity, risk — owning trade-offs measured in staff-years and reporting them in the business's own terms.
- **P6 (Pioneer):** Advises company leadership on where technology changes the business model and is accountable for company-level technology bets paying off.
