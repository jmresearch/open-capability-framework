# Homogenization & product finishing

- **Proposed id:** FBM-13
- **Domain / focus area:** Food & Beverage Manufacturing / Processing
- **Type:** Technical
- **Description:** Operates homogenizers and finishing steps to achieve product structure, texture, and stability targets.
- **Why it's missing:** The role operates high-pressure homogenization and finishing to deliver a physical product structure (fat-globule dispersion, texture, emulsion stability, shelf-stability) — a hands-on process craft tied to pressure/temperature control and physical verification. SCM-07 Manufacturing & production operations is a generic production label that says nothing about achieving product structure through homogenization; SCM-09 Asset & maintenance management covers maintaining the machine, not operating it to a texture/stability target; QA-04 Risk-based quality frames quality decisions but carries none of the finishing competence. None is dairy-process-specific.

## P1–P6 behavioral profile

- **P1 (Assisted):** Runs the homogenizer on the checklist under direction — sets the stage pressures to the sheet, monitors pressure and temperature, and reports abnormal pressure or noise instead of continuing.
- **P2 (Independent):** Operates homogenization and finishing independently across a shift — sets and holds stage pressures for the product, verifies the finished result (fat dispersion/particle target, texture, stability) by the standard check, and manages normal product changes.
- **P3 (Proficient):** Troubleshoots the homogenizer and finishing steps for the line — diagnoses pressure loss, valve/seat wear, cavitation, and off-target texture or separation, restores product structure to spec, and coaches operators on holding the finish through variation.
- **P4 (Expert):** Sets the homogenization and finishing procedure the line follows — defines the pressure/temperature setpoints and verification tests per product that deliver the structure, texture, and stability targets, and standardizes the setup across operators and shifts.
- **P5 (Authority):** Governs product structure and finishing for the department — owns the finishing specifications and stability targets, drives improvement in consistency, energy, and rework, and qualifies changes to homogenization for new or reformulated products.
- **P6 (Pioneer):** Defines homogenization and product-finishing practice beyond the plant — sets structure/stability methods other sites adopt and advances the practice in industry forums.
