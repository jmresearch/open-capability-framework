# Preventive controls & PCQI oversight

- **Proposed id:** FBM-04
- **Domain / focus area:** Food & Beverage Manufacturing / Food Safety Systems
- **Type:** Behavioral
- **Description:** Owning the food-safety plan's preventive controls and the PCQI-level validation, verification, and reanalysis it requires.
- **Why it's missing:** The dairy-plant-operations role needs a preventive-controls qualified individual (PCQI) to own the food-safety plan — process, sanitation, allergen, and supply-chain preventive controls — and the validation, verification, monitoring, corrective action, and reanalysis that framework requires. The nearest catalog capability is SCM-10 (Quality management & control), which owns quality control and nonconformance handling; but the FSMA preventive-controls framework is a food-safety regime, not generic quality control — it requires a qualified individual to validate and reanalyze hazard-based controls on a public-health basis, which SCM-10 does not carry. Within the food-safety family, FBM-03 (HACCP plan & CCP monitoring) is the nearest neighbor, but preventive controls are a distinct, broader regulatory framework layered above HACCP (covering sanitation, allergen, and supply-chain controls, not only CCPs) and require a qualified individual to validate, verify, and reanalyze. (SCM-07 Manufacturing & production operations is generic production.) None cover it.

## P1–P6 behavioral profile

- **P1 (Assisted):** Under direction, carries out an assigned preventive-control monitoring or verification task (a records review, a sanitation verification), records it, and reports the finding to the qualified individual.
- **P2 (Independent):** Independently performs the monitoring and verification activities the food-safety plan assigns for a shift or area, completes the records, and recognizes when a preventive control is out of control.
- **P3 (Proficient):** Owns execution of the preventive controls for an area — verifies monitoring and corrective actions are done and documented, runs the routine verification activities, and coaches others on what each preventive control is protecting against.
- **P4 (Expert):** Serves as a preventive-controls qualified individual for a process — writes and owns the food-safety plan, validates the preventive controls, and defines the verification and corrective-action procedures others follow.
- **P5 (Authority):** Governs the preventive-controls program plant-wide — owns the food-safety plan suite, the validation and verification schedule, and the supplier and supply-chain controls — and leads reanalysis on the required cadence and after any change or failure.
- **P6 (Pioneer):** Advances preventive-controls practice beyond the plant — shapes how food-safety plans are built, validated, and reanalyzed — and is a recognized qualified authority whose approach others adopt.
