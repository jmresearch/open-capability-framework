# Technical debt & risk stewardship

- **Proposed id:** EM-15
- **Domain / focus area:** Engineering Management / Execution & Operations
- **Type:** Behavioral
- **Description:** Surfaces, prices, and manages technical debt and technical risk as an economic portfolio — funding paydown structurally and setting the speed-versus-soundness posture explicitly.
- **Why it's missing:** Present in two ensemble runs (Cunningham anchor). SWE-02 (Code Quality & Maintainability) covers doing quality work; EM-09 covers metrics. Neither covers the leadership act of making debt legible in business terms, defending paydown capacity, and declaring where debt is acceptable.

## P1–P6 behavioral profile

- **P1 (Assisted):** Records known shortcuts and defects when directed; raises debt concerns in planning.
- **P2 (Independent):** Keeps a visible debt register with the cost of carry noted and defends a steady paydown allocation in planning rather than begging quarter by quarter.
- **P3 (Proficient):** Distinguishes debt worth carrying from debt that will detonate, sequences remediation by risk, and prices debt in incident and velocity terms stakeholders accept.
- **P4 (Expert):** Makes debt legible across multiple teams — shared taxonomy, reporting rhythm — and gets cross-team risk (the shared library nobody owns, the half-finished migration) owned and funded.
- **P5 (Authority):** Carries technical risk onto the executive risk register — top exposures known, sized, owned, trending — and lands multi-quarter remediation programs against feature pressure.
- **P6 (Pioneer):** Sets an organization's speed-versus-soundness posture explicitly — where debt is accepted for velocity and where it never is — and holds it under growth pressure.
