# Filling & packaging operation

- **Proposed id:** FBM-14
- **Domain / focus area:** Food & Beverage Manufacturing / Packaging & Cold Chain
- **Type:** Technical
- **Description:** Runs hygienic filling and packaging lines to seal-integrity, fill-weight, date-code, and sanitation targets.
- **Why it's missing:** The role runs the hygienic fill/pack step where product is sealed into consumer packaging — seal integrity, net-content/fill-weight control, date/lot coding accuracy, and line sanitation between formats, a food-contact craft with net-content and food-safety consequence. SCM-07 Manufacturing & production operations is a generic production label with no packaging-line operation depth; SCM-10 Quality management & control owns the in-process quality checks (fill-weight, seal-integrity, coding, defect), but not running the hygienic fill-and-seal itself — operating a food-contact fill/seal to hygienic and net-content spec is the delta here; SCM-08 Production scheduling sequences the run but does not touch seal integrity or coding; WPL-08 Environmental health & safety covers worker safety, not fill/seal quality or hygienic packaging. None is food-safety- or packaging-specific.

## P1–P6 behavioral profile

- **P1 (Assisted):** Runs the filling/packaging line on the checklist under direction — loads material, monitors fill and date-code, pulls the routine seal and fill-weight checks, and stops the line and calls the lead on a seal, code, or fill fault.
- **P2 (Independent):** Operates the filling and packaging line independently across a shift — holds fill weight, seal integrity, and date/lot coding to spec, performs and records the in-process seal and weight checks, and manages normal size/format changeovers and the associated sanitation.
- **P3 (Proficient):** Troubleshoots the filling and packaging line for the area — corrects seal-integrity, fill-weight-variation, coding, and hygienic-fault problems, recovers the line after a jam or contamination event, and coaches operators through changeover and check discipline.
- **P4 (Expert):** Sets the filling and packaging procedure the line follows — defines the fill-weight and seal-integrity control limits, check frequencies, changeover/sanitation steps, and date-code verification others run to, and dials in the line so quality holds across formats and operators.
- **P5 (Authority):** Governs filling and packaging for the department — owns seal-integrity, fill-weight (net-content compliance), coding accuracy, and line-sanitation standards, drives OEE and giveaway/rejection improvement, and defends packaging records in audits.
- **P6 (Pioneer):** Defines hygienic filling and packaging practice beyond the plant — sets seal-integrity and net-content methods other sites adopt and advances the practice in industry forums.
