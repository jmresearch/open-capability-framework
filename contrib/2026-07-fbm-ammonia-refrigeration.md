# Ammonia / CO2 refrigeration operation

- **Proposed id:** FBM-20
- **Domain / focus area:** Food & Beverage Manufacturing / Plant Utilities
- **Type:** Technical
- **Description:** Operating industrial ammonia (or CO2) refrigeration safely and efficiently to hold process and storage temperatures within the limits the product and food-safety plan require.
- **Why it's missing:** The dairy-plant-operations role runs an IIAR-governed industrial refrigeration system — compressors, condensers, evaporators, and a charge of anhydrous ammonia — as the utility that keeps raw milk, process, and cold storage in temperature. The nearest catalog capabilities miss the craft: WPL-02 Building operations & maintenance broadly covers operating and maintaining building systems and facility plant, but not a high-pressure anhydrous-ammonia system run to a mechanical-integrity and process-safety regime — a distinct process-plant competence; WPL-07 Sustainability & energy is adjacent on the energy side but not the operating competence; SCM-09 Asset & maintenance management covers sustaining assets through PM, not the live operating decisions (suction pressure, defrost, charge, oil, purging) that hold product temperature at rate; WPL-08 Environmental health & safety frames the hazard but not the operating competence. None is refrigeration-plant-operation specific.

## P1–P6 behavioral profile

- **P1 (Assisted):** Reads suction/discharge pressures, temperatures, and oil levels off the machine and logs them on the operator round; starts and stops a compressor and switches defrost under direction, and calls a qualified operator on any alarm or unusual reading.
- **P2 (Independent):** Runs the refrigeration plant across a shift on their own — sequences compressors to load, manages defrost and condenser water, watches suction pressure against the setpoint that protects product temperature, and responds to routine high-discharge, low-suction, and oil-level alarms without escalation.
- **P3 (Proficient):** Troubleshoots the system when temperatures drift — diagnoses a fouled condenser, flooded evaporator, non-condensable buildup, or failing compressor from the readings — trims charge, oil, and purge to hold setpoint efficiently, and coaches shift operators through the plant's normal and abnormal operating states.
- **P4 (Expert):** Sets the operating procedures and setpoints others run to — the compressor-sequencing, defrost, purge, and oil-management practice for the plant's areas — tunes the system for energy against a cold-storage and process temperature target, and is the operator called when the plant will not hold temperature.
- **P5 (Authority):** Governs refrigeration operation across the plant — the operating envelope, operator qualification, and the reliability and efficiency program for the system — and drives capacity, energy, and charge-reduction improvements defensible against the IIAR standard and the plant's process-safety obligations.
- **P6 (Pioneer):** Sets refrigeration operating practice beyond the plant — the design-to-operate standards, charge-minimization or CO2/low-charge approaches, and operator-competency models other sites and the industry adopt.
