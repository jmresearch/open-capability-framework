# HACCP plan & CCP monitoring

- **Proposed id:** FBM-03
- **Domain / focus area:** Food & Beverage Manufacturing / Food Safety Systems
- **Type:** Behavioral
- **Description:** Building and running a HACCP plan — monitoring critical control points and executing corrective actions when limits are breached.
- **Why it's missing:** The dairy-plant-operations role needs the food-safety competence of hazard analysis, determining critical control points, monitoring them to critical limits, and taking corrective action on a deviation — the HACCP discipline. The nearest catalog capability is SCM-10 (Quality management & control): it owns quality inspection, control, and nonconformance handling, and genuinely overlaps the monitor-limit / act-on-deviation pattern. But HACCP is a distinct food-safety regime, not generic quality control — it is a structured hazard-analysis method that identifies biological, chemical, and physical food-safety hazards, fixes legally-significant critical limits at defined CCPs, and mandates specific corrective-action, verification, and recordkeeping on a public-health basis; SCM-10 does not carry that hazard-control framework. (WPL-08 Environmental health & safety is worker safety, not product hazard control; SCM-07 Manufacturing & production operations is generic production.) None cover building and running a HACCP plan.

## P1–P6 behavioral profile

- **P1 (Assisted):** Under direction, monitors an assigned critical control point on schedule, records the reading against its critical limit, and immediately notifies a lead and holds product when a limit is exceeded.
- **P2 (Independent):** Independently monitors the critical control points for a line across a shift, recognizes a deviation as it happens, initiates the documented corrective action, and completes the monitoring and corrective-action records.
- **P3 (Proficient):** Owns CCP monitoring for an area — verifies monitoring is happening as written, investigates the cause of a deviation, coaches operators on critical limits and corrective actions, and handles the non-routine deviation without a supervisor.
- **P4 (Expert):** Builds the HACCP plan for a process — conducts the hazard analysis, determines the CCPs and critical limits, writes the monitoring and corrective-action procedures others follow, and leads reassessment when the process changes.
- **P5 (Authority):** Governs the HACCP program across the plant — leads the HACCP team, owns validation of critical limits, the verification and reassessment schedule, and the record-review discipline — and drives systemic corrective action from deviation trends.
- **P6 (Pioneer):** Advances HACCP practice beyond the plant — shapes how hazard analysis and CCP determination are done — and is a recognized authority whose plans and methods others adopt.
