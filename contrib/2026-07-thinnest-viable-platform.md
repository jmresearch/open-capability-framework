# Thinnest viable platform scoping

- **Proposed id:** OPS-31            # next free number in Systems, Infrastructure & Operations
- **Domain / focus area:** Systems, Infrastructure & Operations / Operating Mindset
- **Type:** Behavioral
- **Description:** Scopes an internal platform to the smallest set of APIs, tools, docs, and services that accelerates its consumers - making explicit build-buy-adopt decisions and retiring surface area that no longer earns its maintenance cost.
- **Why it's missing:** The platform-engineering canonical role record (roles/platform-engineering) needs a ratable competency for platform sizing discipline (Team Topologies' "thinnest viable platform"). Nearest existing ids: OPS-27 (platform & developer-experience engineering) covers building paved paths and self-service platforms but not the scoping/portfolio discipline of what to build, buy, wrap, or retire; PM-04 (product strategy & positioning) is market-facing product strategy, not internal-platform boundary management; PROC-01 (sourcing strategy) covers supplier selection, not engineering scope judgment.

## P1-P6 behavioral profile

- **P1 (Assisted):** Checks for an existing tool, service, or open-source option before writing new code and raises what they find; asks whether a proposed task serves a real user need.
- **P2 (Independent):** Proposes wrapping or configuring an existing service instead of building new when it meets the need, writing the comparison down and defending removal of unused features in their own component.
- **P3 (Proficient):** Keeps a whole capability at its thinnest viable size - makes explicit, documented build-buy-adopt decisions, ships the thinnest version that serves proven demand, and retires surface area whose maintenance cost exceeds its value.
- **P4 (Expert):** Arbitrates overlap and sprawl across teams - merges duplicate offerings, cuts under-used surface, and sets the bar for what deserves to be shared platform at all.
- **P5 (Authority):** Shapes the organization's platform boundary - what the platform owns versus product teams versus vendors - and drives the organization to shed what it should not own.
- **P6 (Pioneer):** Sets the build-buy-adopt principles an entire company references, keeping company-scale platform investment proportional to the delivery it unlocks.
