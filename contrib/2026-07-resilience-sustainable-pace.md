# Resilience & sustainable pace

- **Proposed id:** LI-10
- **Domain / focus area:** Leadership & Influence / Personal Leadership
- **Type:** Behavioral
- **Description:** Sustains effectiveness under prolonged pressure, recovers from setbacks, and designs sustainable pace into how teams operate rather than modeling heroics.
- **Why it's missing:** Workbook-sourced (Loehr & Schwartz; burnout research). No catalog capability covers energy management or pace-setting; EM-03 (Team health & engagement) touches team wellbeing but not the leader's own resilience or the operating-rhythm design that protects pace at scale.

## P1–P6 behavioral profile

- **P1 (Assisted):** Maintains composure through normal setbacks; recognizes their own overload and asks for help.
- **P2 (Independent):** Stays effective and steady under pressure — recovers from setbacks, manages their own energy, and models a sustainable pace others learn from watching.
- **P3 (Proficient):** Holds up through prolonged pressure — incident sieges, crunch, organizational turbulence — helping the team stay resilient and refusing burnout-driven decisions.
- **P4 (Expert):** Builds resilience into how multiple teams operate — on-call health, recovery after pushes, load balancing — and repairs burnout risk at scale.
- **P5 (Authority):** Stewards organizational resilience — capacity buffers, crisis rotation, energy management for senior leaders — and holds the line when business pressure argues for permanent crunch.
- **P6 (Pioneer):** Shapes a resilient, sustainable culture across an organization; the organization absorbs shocks without breaking people, and the practice is referenced beyond it.
