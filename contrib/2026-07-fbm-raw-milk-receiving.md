# Raw milk receiving & grading

- **Proposed id:** FBM-10
- **Domain / focus area:** Food & Beverage Manufacturing / Raw Material Handling
- **Type:** Technical
- **Description:** Screens, samples, grades, and accepts or rejects incoming raw milk against temperature, drug-residue, sensory, and load-integrity criteria so only conforming raw material enters the process.
- **Why it's missing:** The Dairy Plant Operations role gates the whole plant on the receiving-bay accept/reject decision — antibiotic screening, load temperature, sensory grading, and tanker/seal integrity — a food-safety-critical craft with its own regulatory frame (PMO Grade A raw-milk requirements). SCM-07 Manufacturing & production operations covers generic make/production work but nothing about grading a raw agricultural input or a drug-residue reject decision; SCM-09 Asset & maintenance management is about equipment upkeep, not incoming-material judgment; QA-04 Risk-based quality frames quality decisions abstractly but carries none of the dairy-receiving competence (sampling, screening, hold/reject at the bay). None is food-safety- or dairy-specific.

## P1–P6 behavioral profile

- **P1 (Assisted):** Follows the receiving checklist under a lead's direction — takes the load temperature, pulls the required sample, records the tanker seal and hauler paperwork, and flags anything out of range instead of unloading.
- **P2 (Independent):** Runs receiving independently across a shift — reads temperature, sensory (odor/appearance), and antibiotic-screen results, accepts conforming loads, and rejects or holds off-spec milk against the standing acceptance criteria.
- **P3 (Proficient):** Troubleshoots disputed loads for the bay — resolves ambiguous drug-residue screens with a confirmatory test, judges borderline sensory and temperature calls, traces a rejected load back to source, and coaches new receivers on sampling technique.
- **P4 (Expert):** Sets the receiving and grading procedure the bay follows — writes the acceptance criteria, sampling plan, and reject/hold decision rules, and calibrates the drug-residue and temperature testing so grading calls are consistent across every operator and shift.
- **P5 (Authority):** Governs the raw-milk quality program for the department — owns supplier/hauler quality standards and drug-residue monitoring, sets acceptance policy, drives quality feedback to producers, and defends the receiving records in a PMO/Grade A inspection.
- **P6 (Pioneer):** Sets raw-milk acceptance and grading practice beyond the plant — defines grading and residue-screening methods other sites adopt and represents the practice in industry and regulatory forums.
