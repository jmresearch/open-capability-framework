# Process safety management (PSM/RMP)

- **Proposed id:** FBM-24
- **Domain / focus area:** Food & Beverage Manufacturing / Process Safety
- **Type:** Behavioral
- **Description:** Running the OSHA Process Safety Management and EPA Risk Management Program elements for a highly hazardous process (anhydrous ammonia) — process hazard analyses, mechanical integrity, management of change, operating procedures, and the rest of the covered-process program.
- **Why it's missing:** A dairy plant holding a threshold quantity of anhydrous ammonia is a covered process under OSHA PSM and EPA RMP, obligating a fourteen-element program the plant must run and defend. The nearest catalog capabilities do not cover it: WPL-08 Environmental health & safety is general workplace safety, not the specific PSM/RMP program elements for a highly hazardous chemical; QA-04 Risk-based quality applies risk thinking to product quality, not process safety; SCM-09 Asset & maintenance management covers maintenance but not mechanical integrity as a regulated PSM element with MOC and PHA discipline. None is process-safety-management specific.

## P1–P6 behavioral profile

- **P1 (Assisted):** Follows the covered-process operating procedures as written, completes their part of PSM tasks under direction — operator training sign-off, a mechanical-integrity check, reporting a change rather than making it silently — and knows the process is PSM-covered and why.
- **P2 (Independent):** Executes their PSM responsibilities independently — runs to the operating procedures, completes mechanical-integrity inspections and tests on schedule, raises a management-of-change when conditions change, and contributes operating knowledge to a process hazard analysis.
- **P3 (Proficient):** Owns PSM elements for the process — keeps operating procedures current and validated, leads or drives closure of PHA and incident-investigation action items, administers MOC so changes are reviewed before they happen, and coaches operators on why the program exists.
- **P4 (Expert):** Sets the plant's PSM practice for the covered process — the PHA schedule and methodology, mechanical-integrity program, MOC and pre-startup-safety-review discipline — and is the reference who makes the program defensible in a compliance audit or after an incident.
- **P5 (Authority):** Governs the PSM/RMP program plant-wide — element ownership, compliance-audit and RMP-resubmission cycle, contractor and training requirements, and the metrics that show the program is working — and drives its continuous improvement.
- **P6 (Pioneer):** Sets process-safety practice beyond the site — program models, leading-indicator and process-safety-culture standards, and covered-process approaches other plants and the industry adopt.
