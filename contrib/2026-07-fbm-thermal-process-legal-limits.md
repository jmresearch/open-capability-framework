# Thermal-process legal limits & records

- **Proposed id:** FBM-02
- **Domain / focus area:** Food & Beverage Manufacturing / Thermal Process Control
- **Type:** Behavioral
- **Description:** Establishing and defending the legal thermal-process minima and the records that prove every batch met them.
- **Why it's missing:** The dairy-plant-operations role needs someone to establish the legally-defensible thermal process for each product (the required time/temperature minima) and own the batch records that prove compliance to an inspector — a governance competence distinct from running the machine. SCM-07 (Manufacturing & production operations) is generic production, not setting legal thermal minima; QA-04 (Risk-based quality) is general quality-risk judgment, not establishing a scheduled thermal process and its legally-defensible records; OPS-04 (Reliability & Production Operations) is uptime. FBM-01 operates the pasteurizer; this establishes and defends the standard and the proof. None cover it.

## P1–P6 behavioral profile

- **P1 (Assisted):** Under direction, records the required time and temperature for the product being run against the posted legal minimum, files the charts and logs completely and legibly, and flags a record that does not show the process was met.
- **P2 (Independent):** Independently confirms the running process against the correct legal minimum for each product, completes and reconciles the thermal-process records for a shift, and knows which reading proves the process and which does not.
- **P3 (Proficient):** Owns the thermal-process records for a line or area — verifies every batch's record shows the legal minimum was met, resolves gaps and discrepancies before they age, and coaches operators on why a specific time/temperature is the legal floor.
- **P4 (Expert):** Establishes the documented scheduled thermal process and legal minima others run to — sets the record requirements, the review-of-records routine, and what constitutes a defensible batch record — and is the person who signs that a lot's process is proven.
- **P5 (Authority):** Governs the thermal-process-authority program across the plant — owns the process filings and validations, the record-retention and review standard, and the escalation for a shorted process — and defends the minima and records to a regulator or auditor without gaps.
- **P6 (Pioneer):** Sets thermal-process-legality practice others adopt — advances how legal minima are established, validated, and documented — and is a recognized authority whose recordkeeping standard is emulated.
