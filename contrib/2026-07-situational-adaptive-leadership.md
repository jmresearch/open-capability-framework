# Situational & adaptive leadership

- **Proposed id:** LI-08
- **Domain / focus area:** Leadership & Influence / Personal Leadership
- **Type:** Behavioral
- **Description:** Reads what the person, team, and moment need and flexes leadership approach — directing, coaching, supporting, delegating — from a grounded, authentic center; distinguishes technical from adaptive challenges.
- **Why it's missing:** Sourced from the engineering-leadership workbook (Hersey-Blanchard, Heifetz, authentic leadership). No catalog capability covers style range: LI-01 (Mentorship & Coaching) is one style, LI-05 (Driving Alignment) one outcome. The meta-skill of matching approach to readiness and situation is separately ratable and separately coachable.

## P1–P6 behavioral profile

- **P1 (Assisted):** Recognizes that different people need different support; adjusts approach when prompted by feedback.
- **P2 (Independent):** Matches direction and support to each person's readiness per task — directing, coaching, supporting, or delegating deliberately — and tells technical problems from adaptive ones.
- **P3 (Proficient):** Flexes skillfully across a wide range of people and hard situations without losing a grounded center; refuses to force technical fixes onto adaptive challenges and names the difference.
- **P4 (Expert):** Coaches other leaders to read what a person, team, or moment needs; adapts across diverse teams while staying recognizably consistent in values.
- **P5 (Authority):** Develops adaptive leadership as an organizational discipline — leaders diagnose technical-versus-adaptive at scale, and the leadership style changes when the situation does.
- **P6 (Pioneer):** Shapes an adaptive, grounded leadership culture across an organization — style stays plastic to conditions while values stay fixed; the approach is emulated beyond the organization.
